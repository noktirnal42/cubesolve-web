Solve³ for Android: privacy policy

Updated 30 October 2026. Publisher: FreeRange Labs. Applies to the Android app with package name com.freerangelabs.CubeSolve.

Solve³ processes puzzle photos, camera frames, color recognition and solving on your device. The app has no Internet permission, developer-operated account, advertising, tracking or analytics SDK. FreeRange Labs does not receive your photos, cube states, practice records or Bluetooth events.

Camera and selected photos

Optional camera access is used for face scanning and the camera turn guide. Capture is processed in memory; closing the camera sheet stops capture. Photos you select are opened through the Android system picker. Raw photos and camera frames are not added to your photo library or saved in solution history. You can deny camera access and use manual color entry instead. Change permission in Android Settings.

Local records and backup

The app stores reviewed sticker colors, per-puzzle progress, saved solutions, settings, cube finish profiles, timer results and training progress in its private local storage. Recovery copies may be retained if a saved record cannot be read. Android device backup may include app records according to your device and account settings. This version does not provide Apple iCloud synchronization.

Optional Bluetooth

Experimental GAN Gen2 tools request nearby-device Bluetooth access to discover and connect to a compatible cube. On Android 11 and earlier, Android requires location permission for Bluetooth discovery; Solve³ uses it only for nearby Bluetooth discovery and does not read GPS coordinates or track your location. Pairing is optional. Cube events are processed locally.

Sharing, exports and platform services

Sharing a solution or exporting timer results sends only the text or file you choose to a destination you select through Android. Your recipient or storage provider handles that copy under its own policies. Spoken guidance uses the text-to-speech engine configured on your device; that engine is governed by its provider's settings and privacy terms. Google handles Google Play purchases, distribution and platform services under Google's policies.

Managing your data

You can start a new puzzle and clear saved solutions within the app. To remove all local app records, use Android Settings to clear Solve³ storage or uninstall the app. System backups and previously exported or shared copies are governed by your device settings and chosen recipients.

Support and children

If you contact support, we receive the information you voluntarily send. Avoid including personal information in photos or logs. Solve³ does not intentionally collect personal information from children or other users. Changes that introduce developer data collection will require an updated policy and store disclosures before release.
