Start a solve

Use a six-color 3×3 cube. Open Scan and capture one face at a time. Follow the face-above label to keep the grid upright. Turn the whole cube between captures without twisting any layer. One picture cannot reveal all 54 stickers. Review every suggested sticker and confirm all six faces, then solve.

Camera or colors need help

Use even light without flash, keep fingers off stickers and show a complete face. Automatic corners can be adjusted manually when needed. Confirm the actual center color if the model misreads it. Rotate the grid only when the photo’s orientation is wrong. Camera access can be enabled in iPhone Settings or Mac System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera. Imported photos and manual entry work without camera permission.

Cube settings → Color calibration lists all six center samples. Capture a named missing center without changing the current cube or guide. Calibration activates after all six usable samples exist. Reset or recapture when changing cubes or lighting. A dark or uneven patch requires a retake. Previously missing samples cannot be recreated from sticker labels; take a fresh photo.

Follow the guide

Keep the selected top/front reference. A plain letter is clockwise as viewed directly at that face; an apostrophe reverses it; 2 means a half turn. Preview shows the moving layer without advancing. Make the physical move, then select I made this turn. Use Back only after undoing the physical turn when necessary. Dragging the virtual cube does not move your actual cube or advance the guide. Reset the reference view with the arrow on the cube.

The virtual cube is a predicted state, not a physical AR overlay. Camera turn tracking requires stable, upright before/after views and can decline to confirm ambiguous faces. If it waits, improve lighting and calibration or advance manually after checking the turn. If you lose track, scan the cube again as it is now.

Invalid cube state

Check color counts, orientation, duplicate pieces and all six confirmed faces. If scans are correct, a twisted corner, flipped edge or swapped pieces can make a cube physically unreachable. The solver does not silently change your colors.

Saved work and limits

History and calibration stay on each device. Raw photos are not retained. Up to 30 solutions are stored locally. No iCloud history sync is included. Supported puzzles are six-color 3×3 cubes; other sizes, mirror cubes and picture-center orientation are not supported. Solutions are verified but not guaranteed shortest.
